# United States Report - September 2026

## Trade Ideas

### 1. Wayfair (W) – Housing Meets Consumer

#### Investment Thesis

The housing backdrop no sign of recovery:

- Housing permits have failed to meaningfully accelerate following the sharp post-2022 slowdown.
- New home sales have begun contracting year-over-year during 2026.
- Mortgage activity remains subdued, whilst prices are unsustainably high.


Without a meaningful improvement in housing turnover or household formation, it is difficult to identify a durable source of demand acceleration for large-ticket furniture and home goods purchases.

#### Valuation Disconnect

- Enterprise Value: approximately **$16 billion**
- 1H26 Pretax Income: approximately **$100 million**
- 1H26 Free Cash Flow: approximately **$200 million**

Despite limited earnings power, the market continues to value the business as a future high-growth winner.

#### Why Now?

The stock rallied following a revenue beat and improving margins. However, much of the improvement appears to be driven by tariff rebates, rather than a significant acceleration in underlying demand.

#### Conclusion

Wayfair remains heavily exposed to a housing market that has yet to show convincing signs of a sustained recovery. While management has executed well on costs and margins, the current valuation appears to discount a stronger consumer and housing environment than is currently visible in the data.
